# W40 Problem Candidates

## Candidate 1: Public hearing and deadline monitor
*(Norwegian: overvåke offentlige høringer og frister)*

### Problem
Important municipal hearings and deadlines can be easy to miss. The workflow would detect new public hearings from Hamar municipality, extract the important information, and produce a structured overview.

### Users
Residents, local organisations, and others who want to follow municipal planning and public consultations.

### Public source
Hamar municipality's public-hearing and planning pages ("offentlig ettersyn" / "planer på høring").

### Definition of success
For every detected hearing, the workflow produces one structured record containing the title, deadline, relevant plan or case identifier when available, and source reference, with the extracted facts matching the public source.

## Candidate 2: Political meeting document organiser
*(Norwegian: hente og strukturere politiske møtepapirer)*

### Problem
Political meeting agendas, case documents, and protocols contain useful information but can require significant manual reading and organisation. The workflow would extract the important structure from Hamar municipality's meeting documents.

### Users
Residents, students, local organisations, and others who want to follow municipal political meetings.

### Public source
Hamar municipality's public political meeting calendar and published case documents.

### Definition of success
For a selected meeting, the workflow produces a structured list containing the meeting date, committee, case numbers, case titles, and document references, with every extracted item traceable to the source.

---

## Candidate 3: Plain-language municipal notice converter
*(Norwegian: gjøre kommunal informasjon enklere å forstå / klart språk)*

### Problem
Municipal notices can contain formal or difficult Norwegian. The workflow would rewrite a public Hamar municipal notice in simpler language while preserving important factual information.

### Users
Residents who find formal municipal language difficult, including people with limited Norwegian proficiency.

### Public source
Public notices and consultation pages published by Hamar municipality.

### Definition of success
The workflow produces a plain-language version in which all important dates, deadlines, addresses, amounts, and case or plan identifiers from the source are preserved and automatically checked.
